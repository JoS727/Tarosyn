What exactly is Tarosyn?

Tarosyn is an AI-native tarot and astrology practice that can connect a seeker’s chart, past cards, chosen guide, and private reflections across sessions. The product also includes a reading vault, ritual and journaling paths, Hero Cards, and selective social experiences.

Why raise $777,000?

Focus. The target is intended to protect founder attention and sustain the core AI product team long enough to improve the product, instrument the business, and test repeatable growth—without prematurely building a large organization. A detailed budget and runway model should be reviewed in diligence.

How does Tarosyn make money?

The current offer architecture includes a free first experience, Mystic at $7.99 monthly or $59.99 annually, and optional Luna packs from $2.99 for additional Hero Card creation. The product must still prove conversion, retention, and contribution economics through measured cohorts.

What stage is the company?

Tarosyn is early and the product is live. This public page intentionally does not publish unverified user, revenue, retention, or growth claims. Current metrics and assumptions should be shared privately when they are ready for investor diligence.

What financing terms are being offered?

No security, valuation, return, or closing terms are offered on this page. Any financing would be subject to applicable law, investor qualification where required, definitive documentation, and direct diligence.

What happens after I request the brief?

You will start a direct conversation with the founder about fit, current materials, product access, the planned use of capital, and the appropriate diligence path. Sending an email does not create a commitment for either side.
