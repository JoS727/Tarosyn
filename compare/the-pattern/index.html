Tarosyn vs The Pattern

Short answer: The Pattern tells you what your chart says about you and the people around you. Tarosyn reads something you did — a card you pulled — and remembers the last time you pulled it.

One is a portrait. The other is a conversation.

What The Pattern is

The Pattern describes itself as a personalized astrology app that helps you understand the patterns shaping your life and influencing your relationships, with a section devoted to the dynamics between you and another person. Its output is descriptive: profiles, cycles, connections derived from birth data.

What Tarosyn is

Tarosyn is a tarot practice with memory. You draw a card or a spread and the reading is written against your natal chart, the cards you keep drawing, and what you wrote last time. It does not describe you in the abstract; it interprets a specific draw.

Who should pick which

  • Pick The Pattern if you want an astrological account of yourself and a structured read on a relationship, without drawing anything.
  • Pick Tarosyn if the thing you actually do is pull cards — or if you want to start, and want a reading that explains itself in plain language and remembers what you have drawn.
  • They are complementary. Plenty of people read their chart for context and pull a card for the decision.

Questions people ask

Which is better for understanding a relationship?

The Pattern is built around connection analysis, so if you want a described dynamic between two people, that is its job. Tarosyn suits the other mode: a shared reading you talk through together, with your own reflections kept alongside it.

Does Tarosyn show me my big life cycles?

No — it is not a transit or cycle tracker. Its continuity comes from your readings, not from planetary periods.

Do I need birth details for Tarosyn?

Not to start. Birth data sharpens the chart layer; you can add it whenever you like.
