Support

We're here to help. Whether you have a question about your account, a billing issue, or something isn't working the way it should — reach out and we'll get back to you.

how to get help

The fastest way to reach us is by email. Send your question, screenshot, or feedback to Support@tarosyn.com and a member of the team will follow up.

response time

We aim to respond to every support request within 2 business days. During busy periods it may take a little longer — we appreciate your patience and will always follow up.

For urgent matters such as account compromise or unexpected charges, include "Urgent" in your subject line and we will prioritise your message.

in-app support

You can also reach our AI support assistant, SupportAngel, directly inside the app. SupportAngel can walk you through features, answer common questions, and escalate to the team when needed.
