Legacy plans

Subscriptions purchased under earlier plan names continue to be honored: Seer subscriptions are served as Mystic, and Founder or Grand Master subscriptions are served as Angel. No action is required from existing subscribers.

Billing Cadence & Auto-Renewal

Monthly plans renew every 30 days from the date of initial purchase. Annual plans renew every 12 months from the date of initial purchase. Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period unless you cancel before the renewal date. You will be charged using the same payment method used at initial purchase. For Apple IAP and Google Play subscriptions, renewal is managed by the respective platform and may occur up to 24 hours before the period ends.

Free Trial

New subscribers receive a 14-day free trial (one Moon Turn) when first signing up. A second 14-day trial period is granted when you choose your first paid plan during the trial window. You will not be charged during the free trial period. At the end of the trial, your subscription converts to paid automatically unless cancelled beforehand. Free trials are available to new subscribers only and may not be combined with promotional offers.

Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time via your Subscription settings, or through the App Store / Google Play billing settings. Upon cancellation, your access to paid features continues until the end of the current billing period. No refund is issued for unused days in the current billing period (except where required by applicable law or platform policy). Cancellation takes effect at the next renewal date; no future charges are made after that date. Downgrading to Seeker (Free) removes paid-tier perks at the end of the current paid period.

Price Changes

Tarosyn may change subscription prices at any time. You will be given at least 30 days' advance notice of any price change via email and/or in-app notice. Continued use of your subscription after the effective date of a price change constitutes your acceptance of the new price. If you do not accept a price change you may cancel before it takes effect.

Apple App Store (IAP)

Subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store are billed by Apple Inc. and governed by Apple's Terms of Sale. Auto-renewal can be turned off in your Apple ID account settings at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Refund requests for App Store purchases must be submitted through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Tarosyn cannot issue refunds for IAP transactions. Your App Store subscription can be restored on any device signed into the same Apple ID using the Restore Purchases option on the Subscribe screen.

Google Play (IAP)

Subscriptions purchased through Google Play are billed by Google LLC and governed by Google's Terms of Service. Auto-renewal can be managed in the Google Play app under Subscriptions. Refund requests for Google Play purchases must be submitted through Google at Google Play Help. Tarosyn cannot issue refunds for Play transactions. Purchases can be restored by signing into the same Google account on any Android device or the Tarosyn app.

Web (Stripe)

Subscriptions purchased on the web are processed by Stripe and billed directly to your credit or debit card. You may update your payment method or cancel at any time from your Subscription settings. Web subscription refund requests can be sent to support@tarosyn.com. Refunds are not guaranteed; see our Refund Policy for details. Failed payments may result in a brief grace period before your subscription is cancelled.
