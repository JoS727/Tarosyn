Community Guidelines

Last updated May 31, 2026

Tarosyn is a space for genuine spiritual exploration, reflection, and connection. These Community Guidelines describe the standards that apply to everything seekers share — Chant feed posts, Circle messages, direct messages, comments, profile bios, and uploaded media. They sit alongside our Acceptable Use Policy, which covers broader platform rules including fraud, security abuse, and AI misuse.

1. the spirit of tarosyn

We built Tarosyn for seekers who take their inner life seriously. The community that grows around it should reflect that intention — curious, kind, and respectful of different paths. You don't need to agree with every perspective you encounter, but you do need to treat the people behind them with basic human dignity.

2. content standards

These standards apply across Chant posts, comments, Circle messages, DMs, profile bios, display names, uploaded photos, and any other content you share through the Service.

Allowed:

Personal tarot reflections, spreads, and interpretations Astrology, numerology, and spiritual discussion Creative expression: poetry, journaling, ritual sharing Supportive responses to other seekers Constructive disagreement, expressed respectfully Questions, curiosity, and exploration — including scepticism

Not allowed:

Hate speech or content demeaning people based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or age Harassment, threats, bullying, or targeted abuse directed at any individual Pornographic or sexually explicit content (NSFW), and non-consensual intimate imagery Sexual content involving minors (CSAM) — zero tolerance; immediately reported to authorities Graphic violence or content designed to shock or distress Content that encourages self-harm, suicide, or eating disorders Spam: repetitive, off-topic, or commercial content posted without invitation Impersonating another user, public figure, or Tarosyn staff Sharing another person's private information without consent (doxxing) Illegal content of any kind

3. reporting content

Every piece of user-generated content on Tarosyn — Chant posts, comments, Circle messages, profiles — carries an in-app Report control. Tap it to flag anything that violates these guidelines. You can also report a user's profile from their public page.

Not sure where to start? Our User Reporting Process walks you through finding the Report button on each surface and what happens after you submit.

For urgent matters — imminent harm, CSAM, law-enforcement requests — contact us directly at Support@tarosyn.com.

4. moderation

Tarosyn moderators review every in-app report within 24 hours. Depending on severity we may remove content, restrict features, suspend, or permanently ban accounts. Decisions are at Tarosyn's sole discretion.

If you believe an action was taken in error, you can contest it through our Appeals Process, or email Support@tarosyn.com with the subject line "Moderation Appeal" and relevant context.
