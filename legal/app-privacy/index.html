App Privacy Disclosures

Last updated May 31, 2026

App stores require us to declare the categories of data Tarosyn collects, how that data is used, and whether it is linked to your identity or used for tracking. This page keeps a public, human-readable record of those declarations so you always know exactly what we submitted.

No tracking for advertising. Tarosyn does not use any data to track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not share data with data brokers or advertising networks.
