SHORT (≈ 45 WORDS)

Tarosyn is an AI-powered tarot, astrology, and spiritual platform for modern seekers. Available on iOS, Android, and web, Tarosyn blends ancient wisdom with generative AI to deliver personalised readings, daily card revelations, birth charts, and a vibrant spiritual community.

LONG (≈ 120 WORDS)

Founded in 2024, Tarosyn LLC is a US-based company building AI-powered tools for spiritual exploration. The Tarosyn app combines a full 78-card Rider-Waite tarot system with eight distinct AI mystic personas, an astrology engine powered by Swiss Ephemeris, numerology, dream interpretation, and photo readings — all wrapped in a cinematic dark-themed mobile experience. Beyond solo practice, Tarosyn hosts a rich social layer: a TikTok-style Chant Feed, private Circles communities, synastry chart comparisons, and shared spiritual milestones. Luna credits power a built-in economy of card packs, relics, and artisan merch. Rated 17+ on the App Store and Google Play, Tarosyn is free to download with optional subscription tiers (Seer, Creator, Founder) for deeper features.
