01 / THE CHARTER

Ritual tech for modern mystics.

Tarosyn combines AI-powered tarot readings, birth-chart insight, guided ritual, journaling, and community into an experience designed to become more meaningful over time. The standard is mystical but grounded, emotionally intelligent, and genuinely useful.

Personal, not generic

Guidance should remember context and deepen with a seeker’s journey.

Beautiful, not performative

Design and language should feel elevated without empty mysticism.

Reflective, not absolute

Tarosyn supports self-reflection; it does not promise supernatural certainty.

04 / HOW THIS STAYS LIVING

Facts over fog.

This page is intentionally a public snapshot, not a launch-promise machine. Material product, roadmap, or positioning changes are reviewed against the codebase and private operating record before being published here. Internal feature, release, and technical records stay in Tarosyn Live; secrets never live in a repository.

  • Public copy avoids private user, security, credential, and infrastructure details.
  • Local work or a committed branch is never called “live” until the public deployment is verified.
  • Major decisions are captured with ownership and rationale.
