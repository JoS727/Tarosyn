The short answer

What is Tarosyn?

Tarosyn is a personalized tarot and astrology app that remembers your readings. Each reading combines your natal chart, the cards you have drawn before, and reflections you have written yourself — so guidance builds over time instead of starting from zero every day.

What actually happens in a reading

  1. Your chart sets the context. Date, time and place of birth are used to calculate a natal chart. You can begin with less information if you prefer.
  2. You draw. A daily card, or a spread you choose. Tarosyn keeps a deck with its own illustrated card faces, and some cards can be forged into a personal hero card using Kuu$, the in-app currency.
  3. The reading answers in your context. The interpretation is written against your chart, the cards that keep returning for you, and what you wrote last time — not against a generic card dictionary.
  4. You reflect, and it is kept. Notes and readings live in your vault. The next reading can draw on them, which is what makes the practice cumulative rather than episodic.

How it differs from a horoscope app or a card of the day

Most tarot and astrology apps are excellent at the daily prompt: one card, one horoscope, done. The next day starts clean. Tarosyn is built around the opposite premise — that a reading is worth more when it knows what came before it.

  • Continuity, not repetition. Past cards and your own reflections are part of the next reading, so the app can point at a repeated card or a theme you have circled twice.
  • Two lenses at once. Tarot supplies the question; your chart supplies the timing and temperament. Readings use both rather than picking one tradition.
  • Reflection, not prediction. Tarosyn offers themes, questions and possibilities. It is explicit that it does not know your future.
  • Private by default. Your vault is yours. Sharing and community features are choices, never the price of participation.

Where Tarosyn runs

Tarosyn is web-first: it runs at tarosyn.app in any modern mobile or desktop browser, so a fix or a new reading ships without waiting on an app-store review. Native iOS and Android builds are in preparation and currently listed as coming soon.

Who it is for

People who already pull a card or read their horoscope and want the practice to accumulate; people who keep a paper tarot journal and would rather it were searchable; and people who are curious but do not want to learn 78 card meanings first. No prior tarot or astrology knowledge is required — every card and connection is explained in plain language.

What it is not

  • Not a prediction engine. No guaranteed outcomes, no claims about your future.
  • Not medical, legal or financial advice. Readings are for reflection and entertainment, and are not a substitute for professional help.
  • Not a social network you are pushed into. Sharing is opt-in.

Common questions

Is Tarosyn a tarot app or a horoscope app?

Both, in one reading. The question comes from a tarot draw; the context comes from your natal chart. Horoscopes on tarosyn.com are a separate, free daily page — the app is the reading practice.

Does it remember what I wrote?

Yes. Saved readings and reflections are part of your vault, and future readings can draw on them so the practice builds continuity.

Can I use it without giving birth details?

Yes. Birth data sharpens the chart layer, but you can begin with less context and add it later.

How do I cancel a paid plan?

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Annual plans include a 14-day free trial, with billing terms shown before you subscribe.

What is Kuu$?

Kuu$ is Tarosyn's in-app currency. Paid tiers include a monthly allowance, and packs can be bought separately for things like hero-card forging.
