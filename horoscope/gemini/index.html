Gemini Horoscope

Thursday, November 26, 2026

Two minds, and neither of them wants to finish.

The reading. Gemini days usually go wrong in the same place: the beginning is electric and the middle is abandoned. Today is built to interrupt that. The thing you already started is worth more attention than the thing you have not started yet — not because it is better, but because finishing is the skill you are actually practising. Your instinct will argue that returning to old work wastes a high-energy day. That instinct is the reason four things are unfinished.

Love and friendship: air signs talk their way into intimacy and then talk their way out of it. If a conversation has been circling for a week, today is the day for the plain version instead of the clever one. The clever version is how a Gemini stays at arm's length while appearing fully present.

The week around it. A pattern worth watching this week: the number of things you describe as almost done. Almost done is a state of rest for Gemini, not a state of work — it is where a piece goes when it is finished enough to talk about and unfinished enough to still be free. Count them. If the number is above three, the week's job is decided for you and it has nothing to do with anything new.

Today's focus. Thursday brings a message worth answering slowly. Read it twice before replying once.

Today's card: The Hanged Man, reversed

Upright it is the deliberate pause that reveals a situation. Reversed, the pause has become the problem — clarity already arrived and nothing has been done with it. Read it as an invitation to stop waiting, not as a promise that something will change.

One instruction

Set twenty-five minutes on the oldest unfinished thing in your drafts. When it ends, decide: finish it tomorrow, or delete it. Leaving it undecided is the expensive option.
