Pisces Horoscope

Thursday, November 26, 2026

Absorbs the room, then wonders why the mood is not theirs.

The reading. Pisces has no reliable boundary between what is felt and what is received, which is the source of both the empathy and the exhaustion. Today, run a simple test on your current mood: would you have this feeling if you had spent the last two days alone? If the honest answer is no, the feeling belongs to somebody else and you have been carrying it as a favour nobody asked for.

Creativity: the work is closer to finished than it feels. Pisces tends to hold pieces in a state of potential because potential cannot be judged. Finish it and let it be judged.

The week around it. Something in the week will ask you to describe a feeling to somebody who wants a fact. Resist the urge to convert the feeling into a fact to make it acceptable — a report, a timeline, a conclusion you do not actually have. Say the vague true thing once, plainly, and then be quiet. Most of the pressure you feel to have this resolved comes from wanting the conversation to be easier for the other person, which is generosity doing the work of avoidance.

Today's focus. Thursday is good for water, music, and anything that moves feeling without requiring words.

Today's card: The High Priestess

A seated figure with a veil behind her and a scroll half-hidden. It is the card of knowledge that has not been spoken yet. For Pisces the warning is specific: the scroll stays hidden because you have not looked at it, not because it is secret.

One instruction

Write down what you already know about the situation you are calling unclear. Do not resolve it. Just write it and look at it.
