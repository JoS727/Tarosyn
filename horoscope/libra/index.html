Libra Horoscope

Saturday, November 21, 2026

Can see every side of a decision, which is why the decision is late.

The reading. Libra's fairness is genuine, and it is also the most reliable way to avoid choosing. Every side looks defensible because you are good at seeing what is defensible in it. Today, notice how many open questions in your life are being kept open not by difficulty but by symmetry: the two options are close enough that choosing either would be fine, and staying undecided is the only option that definitely costs you.

Relationships: a boundary stated plainly is kinder than a compromise nobody wanted. The Libra move is to find the version everyone can live with. The honest move today is to say which version you can live with.

The week around it. This week will produce at least one moment where two people you like want incompatible things and both expect you to agree. You will be tempted to be the reasonable one, since you are better at it than anyone in the room. Being reasonable is not the same as being honest, and the cost of the difference gets paid later, by you, in the form of an arrangement you cannot remember agreeing to. Say which one you agree with. Not who is nicer. Which one.

Today's focus. Saturday wants novelty that is not a new project. A different route, a different room, a different person.

Today's card: Justice

A sword and a scale, and the sword is not decorative. The scale weighs; the sword decides. Libra tends to be excellent at the first and reluctant at the second, which is why the card is assigned to the sign as a corrective rather than a compliment.

One instruction

Pick one open decision and close it before the day ends. It does not have to be the perfect option — it has to stop being undecided.
