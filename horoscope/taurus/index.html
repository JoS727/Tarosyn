Taurus Horoscope

Thursday, November 26, 2026

Slow to move, slower to be wrong.

The reading. Taurus is the sign that gets accused of stubbornness by people who are simply in a hurry. Today the argument is on your side — the thing you refused to abandon is starting to show why. That said, there is a difference between holding a position and refusing to look at it, and today is a good day to check which one you are doing. Re-examine the thing you are defending. If it survives the look, defend it harder.

Money: a good day to price something honestly rather than competitively. The temptation is to undercut to feel safe. The Taurus advantage is patience — you can afford to wait for the right buyer in a way your competitors cannot.

The week around it. There is a conversation coming this week that you have been pre-writing in your head for longer than you would admit. Notice that you have drafted six versions and sent none. The draft is not the problem — the expectation that the other person will react precisely as you scripted it is. Say the version that leaves room for them to surprise you, and you will find the conversation shorter than the preparation.

Today's focus. Thursday is good for a small repair — the hinge, the invoice, the habit that has drifted three degrees.

Today's card: The Emperor

Structure, provision, the throne that is earned by building rather than taking. It is the Tarot card most aligned with Taurus temperament, and it comes with a warning: a throne becomes a cage when nobody is allowed to move the furniture.

One instruction

Re-open one decision you made months ago and test whether it is still true, not whether it is still yours.
