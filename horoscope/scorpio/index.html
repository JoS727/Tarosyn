Scorpio Horoscope

Saturday, November 28, 2026

Prefers the true answer to the comfortable one.

The reading. Scorpio does not fear the dark, which means the sign is often the only one in the room willing to name what is actually going on. The cost of that skill is that you rarely stop looking — including at things that have already answered you. Today, ask which of your current investigations is a search and which is a habit of searching. One of them has an answer you have not permitted yourself to accept.

Money: you are good at leverage and bad at letting a position go. Something you are holding — a subscription, a stake, an account — is charging you rent to keep it. Cancel one thing you keep out of refusal to be wrong.

The week around it. There is something you keep testing and re-testing, and the week is a good time to notice that no result would settle it. Testing is not the same as investigating; testing repeats, investigating concludes. If you have already run the same scenario twice, the third run is not evidence, it is reassurance. Very little of what you actually need is reassurance, and the part that is should come from somewhere other than the thing in question.

Today's focus. Saturday is for the people who do not require you to perform. Spend the day with one of them.

Today's card: Death

The most misread card in the deck. It is not death; it is the end of something that needed to end, and the refusal to end it is what makes it feel like death. Whatever you have been keeping alive on principle is the thing the card is pointing at.

One instruction

Close one account, thread, or arrangement you keep purely because closing it would mean admitting it ended badly.
