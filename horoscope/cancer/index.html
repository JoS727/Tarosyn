Cancer Horoscope

Friday, November 20, 2026

Remembers everything, says almost none of it.

The reading. Cancer holds a grudge the way a house holds heat — quietly, and long after the fire is out. Today is a good day to find one of those stored things and check its temperature. Most of what we carry from a slight is not the slight itself but the version of ourselves we were when it happened. That person is not the person reading this. Some of what you are still protecting yourself against already ended.

Home: a small physical change will do more for your mood than a large emotional one — the difference between rearranging a room and rearranging your life is that the first one finishes today.

The week around it. This week has an ending in it that you have not scheduled. Something in your calendar or your habits is running on a momentum that stopped being true a while ago, and you are the only one maintaining it. Ask what would actually break if you stopped. The answer is usually a silence someone else has been relying on, and a silence that lasts is a thing you are allowed to stop paying for.

Today's card: The Moon

The Moon is the sign's own card: tides, instinct, and the way a feeling can be accurate and still not be information. It asks a specific question — is this intuition, or is this memory wearing intuition's clothes?

One instruction

Name the one thing you are still braced for. Then check whether it is happening now, in this week, or whether you are bracing against a year that is over.
