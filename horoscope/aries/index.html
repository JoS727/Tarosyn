Aries Horoscope

Saturday, November 28, 2026

The sign that would rather start badly than wait well.

The reading. Aries days go wrong at the second step, not the first. The start is never the problem — the ignition is genuine, and the momentum is real. What breaks is the middle: the part where the thing stops being exciting and starts being a task. Today is a middle day. Whatever you began this week and quietly stopped touching is the only thing worth your attention, and it will feel slower than you want.

Work: the instinct today is to open something new to escape the drag of something old. Notice that instinct as a preference for novelty, not a judgement about the work. One more hour on the thing already moving is worth more than a fresh start.

The week around it. The week is asking a question about patience, which is not a virtue Aries is given much credit for. Watch how you react when someone tells you to wait — the reaction is the information. If the resistance is physical, that is impatience, and impatience is fine as long as it is directed at the work rather than at the people. If the resistance is closer to boredom, the thing may genuinely be finished and you are simply avoiding the moment of finding out.

Today's focus. Saturday wants movement that has no purpose. Walk somewhere you have no reason to be.

Today's card: The Chariot

Two horses, one driver. The card is a warning about speed without direction — the Aries failure mode in one image. You are not short of drive today. You are short of an agreed destination.

One instruction

Write down the one sentence that describes what finishing looks like. If you cannot write it, that is why you stopped.
