Sagittarius Horoscope

Saturday, November 21, 2026

Aims far, and hates the part where the arrow has to be fetched.

The reading. Sagittarius is the sign most likely to be right about the direction and least likely to stay for the logistics. The vision is not the problem today; the follow-through is. Somewhere in the last month you pointed at something genuinely good and then did not open the spreadsheet it needed. The good news is that the direction was correct. The work today is the unglamorous second half, and it will feel beneath you, and it is not.

Work and study: the temptation is a new course, a new framework, a new city. Ask what the current project needs, and notice how often the answer is a boring document rather than more inspiration.

The week around it. The week has an ending that will feel like an interruption because it arrives during something you were enjoying. Allow it. Sagittarius tends to extend good things into bad ones by refusing to let them finish on time — the trip that runs three days too long, the project that becomes an obligation. When something is complete, the best available action is to be the person who notices it first rather than the one who is told.

Today's focus. Saturday wants movement that has no purpose. Walk somewhere you have no reason to be.

Today's card: The Fool

A step off a cliff with a small bag and no plan. It is the correct card for beginnings and the wrong card for the fifth beginning in a row. The Fool is only wise when it is not being used as an exit.

One instruction

Book the boring, necessary appointment — the one that exists purely to move a project forward — before you allow yourself the next interesting idea.
